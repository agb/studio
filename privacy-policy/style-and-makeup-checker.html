Privacy Policy

Last updated · January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Style and Makeup Checker ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our App, we collect the following information:

  • Camera Images: The App requires access to your device's camera to capture photos for style and makeup analysis. These images are processed locally on your device and are never stored remotely or transmitted to external servers.
  • Analysis Data: The App processes your images to extract makeup and style information, including but not limited to:
    • Facial features and structure
    • Makeup application details
    • Style and fashion attributes
    • Color analysis and recommendations
  • AI Processing: To provide analysis services, your images may be sent to third-party AI services (including ChatGPT and similar AI platforms) for processing. However, images are not stored by these services and are only used for real-time analysis.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide style and makeup analysis services
  • To generate personalized recommendations based on your photos
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience
  • To process and analyze makeup and style data for recommendations

3. Data Storage

All images and photos captured through the App are stored exclusively on your device's local storage. We do not maintain any remote servers or cloud storage for your images. Your photos remain under your complete control and are never uploaded to our servers or third-party storage services.

Analysis results and style recommendations may be stored locally on your device to provide you with a history of your analyses and improve your experience with the App.

4. Data Processing

Makeup and style information extracted from your images may be processed and analyzed to provide you with:

  • Personalized style recommendations
  • Makeup suggestions and tips
  • Color palette recommendations
  • Fashion style analysis

This processing occurs either locally on your device or through secure, temporary connections to AI processing services. No processed data is permanently stored by third-party services.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party AI services (such as ChatGPT) to process your images for analysis purposes. These services:

  • Process images only for the duration necessary to provide analysis
  • Do not store your images permanently
  • Are bound by their own privacy policies and terms of service

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party services to understand how they handle data during processing.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access all images stored locally on your device through your device's file system
  • Deletion: You can delete any images or analysis data stored locally on your device at any time
  • Control: You can revoke camera permissions at any time through your device settings
  • Opt-out: You can stop using the App at any time, and all locally stored data will remain on your device until you manually delete it

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data:

  • All images are stored locally on your device, protected by your device's security measures
  • No remote storage means reduced risk of data breaches
  • Secure connections are used when communicating with AI processing services
  • Data is processed in a temporary, non-persistent manner

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
