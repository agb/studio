Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Sleep Assistant: Sleep Now, also displayed as Sleep AI (the “App”), provided by AGB Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App or visit these public information pages. It describes the current App version, 0.1.1.

The App provides optional general-wellness suggestions for adults. It is not a healthcare service or a clinical research service. Our Terms of Service explain these limits.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The current App operates locally. Depending on the features you use, it stores the following information on your device:

  • Your adult confirmation and general-wellness acknowledgement
  • Your timezone, usual wake time, and chosen bedtime targets
  • Bedtime plans, routine steps, and their completion state
  • Alarm schedules, recurrence settings, and optional alarm labels
  • Optional morning check-ins: your estimated sleep and wake times, sleep-quality and energy ratings, selected factors, and any note you choose to enter
  • Optional dietary, allergy, intolerance, routine, and audio preference flags used to filter suggestions
  • App preferences and progress, including notification-primer completion and lock-screen preview choices

Sleep records, notes, and health-adjacent preferences may be sensitive. Optional preferences and check-ins can be skipped. Avoid entering medical records or information about other people in notes. Your timezone comes from device settings or your selection, rather than GPS or precise-location access.

2. How Information Is Used

Local information is used to remember your choices, show and adapt bedtime routines, schedule the alarms and reminders you request, display your history, and summarize your self-reported patterns. These summaries are personal reflections, not medical measurements, diagnoses, or research findings. We do not receive your local sleep records or preference flags through the current App.

3. AI, Accounts, and Online Features

Despite its name, the current App uses built-in routine suggestions and local assistant responses. It does not send your sleep records, notes, or preferences to an external AI provider or use them to train AI models. There is no active developer-operated account, cloud-sync, or remote AI service connected to this version, and no Apple or Google sign-in is required.

If a future version introduces online processing, we will update this policy to identify the information, purposes, service providers, retention, and applicable choices before that processing begins. This policy does not authorize undisclosed future data collection.

4. Permissions and Notifications

Optional notifications and alarm features use the platform permissions needed to deliver reminders and alarms. Android may require notification and exact-alarm permission; supported iOS versions may request alarm authorization. Android can restore scheduled alarms after a device restart. You can manage permissions in system settings and choose the available notification-preview setting in the App.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, HealthKit, Health Connect, or wearable health data. It does not passively detect sleep stages, snoring, breathing, or medical conditions. Operating-system services handle alarm and notification delivery under the platform’s own privacy practices.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Sharing

The current App has no advertising or active product-analytics service, does not collect advertising identifiers, and does not sell or share your local records for advertising or health targeting. It does not collect payment-card details. App-store distribution and any diagnostics you separately choose to share with Apple or Google are governed by their policies and your platform settings.

These legal pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an external link subjects that visit to the destination’s privacy practices.

6. Support Messages

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot the App, and handle privacy or legal requests. Our email service provider processes those communications to deliver and store them. Send only what is needed; please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or medical records.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal, security, or dispute-handling obligations, then delete it or remove identifying information. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect lawful rights. Support messages are not used for clinical research or advertising.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Security

Local records remain until you edit or delete them using available App controls, clear the App’s storage where your system permits it, or remove the App. Cancel unwanted alarms and reminders using the available controls. We cannot remotely inspect or delete information that exists only on your device.

The App uses an encrypted local database and platform secure storage for its encryption key. Android app backup is disabled. On iOS, encrypted database copies may remain in device backups and secure Keychain entries can survive app removal; manage backups using your device’s controls. No storage system is completely secure. Protect your device and any copies or exports you make.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent where it is used; or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify it. Local App records must be managed on your device because we do not hold a remote copy in the current version.

Where applicable, we process support information to respond to your request, pursue legitimate interests in providing support and securing the service, or comply with legal obligations. Website and email providers may process information outside your country under their applicable privacy terms and transfer safeguards.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect children’s information through our support channel. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will publish changes at this URL and update the date above. For material changes to data practices, we will provide an appropriate notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law before beginning the new processing.
